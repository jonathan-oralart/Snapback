How to check

  1. Start a Snapback recording of this window.
  2. Press the pad, hold it about half a second, let go. Do it a few times, with the left and right buttons.
  3. Stop, then step through the frames with ← and →. The clock changes every frame, so every frame is in the recording.
  4. The filled ring should first appear on the frame the pad turns red, or a frame or two before it: the browser shows a click on the next screen refresh, so the page lags the click slightly.
  5. The hollow ring should first appear on the frame the pad turns green again, again up to two frames early.
  6. For a number: on the first frame with a ring, compare the clock with the time logged for that press. The clock should be 0–35 ms later. Much more, or a ring after the pad changes, means the timing is off.
